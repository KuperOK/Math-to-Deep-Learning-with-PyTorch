# Math to Deep Learning with PyTorch

A beginner-friendly series of Jupyter notebooks that explains the core mathematics behind neural networks and connects each concept directly to its implementation in **PyTorch**.

The goal is not only to show *how to write PyTorch code*, but also to explain **what happens mathematically during forward propagation, backpropagation, and model training**.

The notebooks gradually move from simple derivatives to building and training complete neural networks.

## What You Will Learn

The series covers:

- derivatives and partial derivatives;
- gradients and the chain rule;
- PyTorch `autograd`;
- forward and backward propagation;
- activation functions: Sigmoid, Tanh, ReLU;
- neurons, layers, weight matrices, and biases;
- building models with `nn.Module`;
- loss functions: MSE, MAE, BCE, Cross-Entropy;
- Gradient Descent, SGD, Momentum, and Adam;
- learning rate and parameter updates;
- complete neural-network training pipelines.

Each notebook combines:

- short mathematical explanations;
- visualizations;
- PyTorch code;
- manual calculations before automation;
- small experiments and exercises.

## Who Is This For?

This series is intended for students and beginners in **Machine Learning / Deep Learning** who already know basic Python and want to understand what PyTorch is doing under the hood.
